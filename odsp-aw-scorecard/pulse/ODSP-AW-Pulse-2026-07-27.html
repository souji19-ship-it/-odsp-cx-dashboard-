what changed - the highlights
ODSP tool calls on Cowork kept climbing
Cowork ODSP tool calls rose 1.9M → 2.1M (▲ 12.6%) and tasks 207.4K → 239.7K (▲ 15.6%), with ODSP active users 84.4K → 86.8K (▲ 2.9%); tool success eased 95.8% → 95.7% (▼ 0.1pp).
Copilot Studio ODSP knowledge search surged
SharePoint knowledge searches jumped 282.4K → 431.1K (▲ 52.6%) and ODSP-active users 13.1K → 15.6K (▲ 18.8%), tenants 4.3K → 4.6K (▲ 8.2%) - though connector tool calls fell 202.6K → 116.9K (▼ 42.3%).
ODSP stayed deeply embedded in Cowork
ODSP reached ~43% of Cowork users and ~57% of Cowork tenants, and drove ~22% of all Cowork tasks.
