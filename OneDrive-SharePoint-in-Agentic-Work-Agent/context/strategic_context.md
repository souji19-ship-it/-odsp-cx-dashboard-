## Measurement Philosophy (from Master Deck)

> Focus on Outcomes, Not Metrics Overload. Keep it simple — identify the one metric
> that truly matters (e.g., thumbs-down rate) and avoid cluttering the scorecard
> with unnecessary data. Show Impact Clearly — demonstrate how your work moves the
> needle on business priorities like retention, usage, satisfaction.
